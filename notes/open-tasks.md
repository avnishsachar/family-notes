## Call the same day if

- Readings persistently at or above 160/100 despite rest and paracetamol.
- One calf swollen, hot, red or tender compared with the other.
- Fever 37.5 or above.
- Confusion clearly worse than the day before.
- Headache that depends on position (worse upright, better flat = over-drainage; worse flat or on waking = under-drainage).

## Emergency department, do not wait

- Breathlessness, chest pain, oxygen below 94, coughing blood, fainting.
- Drowsy or hard to wake.
- Fever with headache or vomiting.
- Sudden severe headache, new weakness, trouble speaking or seeing.
- Redness, swelling or leaking along the shunt tubing or wounds.
